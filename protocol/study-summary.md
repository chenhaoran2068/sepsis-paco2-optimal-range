# Study Summary

## Full study identity

**Title:** Daily Time-Weighted PaCO2 and 28-Day Mortality in ICU Sepsis: A Multicohort Retrospective Study.

**Design:** Retrospective multicohort observational study of ICU patients with sepsis.

**Data sources:** MIMIC-IV 3.1, AmsterdamUMCdb 1.0.2, the Fourth People's Hospital of Xuzhou cohort, and the First People's Hospital of Lianyungang cohort.

**Exposure:** Daily time-weighted arterial carbon dioxide partial pressure during ICU days 1 to 7.

**Outcome:** 28-day all-cause mortality.

## Interpretation boundary

The study assesses observational associations and longitudinal risk patterns. Its model-estimated nadir and near-minimum risk range are statistical landmarks, not treatment targets. No causal treatment-effect claim or bedside control recommendation follows from this code package or the study results.

## Public package boundary

This candidate contains a synthetic demonstration of the selected analysis structure. It does not contain the study datasets, source-data adapters, manuscript result files, or a runnable authorized source-data workflow.
