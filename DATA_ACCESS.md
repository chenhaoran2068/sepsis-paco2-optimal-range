# Data Access

## Distributed with this candidate

No source, participant-level, or derived study data are distributed. The only
data created by the demonstration are generated at runtime by
`code/R/generate_demo_data.R`. They are newly generated synthetic records and
are not renamed, sampled, perturbed, or transformed study records.

## Restricted study data

MIMIC-IV 3.1 is a controlled-access public dataset available to credentialed
users through PhysioNet under its applicable requirements. AmsterdamUMCdb 1.0.2
is subject to its applicable access route and license terms. The Chinese cohort
data are not publicly available because institutional data-use, ethics, and
privacy restrictions apply.

This repository does not convey authorization to access, use, download,
combine, or redistribute any of these datasets. A person seeking to perform an
authorized source-data analysis must independently satisfy the data provider and
institutional requirements and must use a separately reviewed, authorized
workflow. Source-data extraction adapters are deliberately outside this public
candidate.
